# Brasswake story bible

Continuity for the 2026-11-22 story revision. The runtime rules and release evidence live in `02_GAMEPLAY.md` and the implementation plan. The writing standard lives in `04_WRITING_STYLE.md`.

## Synopsis

Tamsin, a deckhand on the Marigold, helps pull a stranger from the Brass Sea on June 14. The stranger recovers during the ship's return to Tallyport and tells Tamsin about their old work. Tamsin wants them to land with a friend and a way to earn money. She introduces them to Odalys Brine, who uses a small copy of the ship manifest to teach them SQL. The stranger starts with a useful job on the docks and tries the same kind of query on a training dummy. Old skills explain what the stranger cares about and which tools they start with. They still have to learn the way skilled work draws on the Ledger here.

Corin Hale died in the Bilge Vaults while trying to recover the lost ships register. He was Tallyport's champion and Odalys had taught his mentor, Ilva Thorne. With the Trials coming up, Tallyport needs a candidate to keep its voice on the council. Odalys offers practical help and a chance to learn. She knows what it cost her last pupil's teacher to send him into danger. The stranger can help at the harbor and eventually represent the town. Tamsin returns to her ship after helping them start out and rejoins the story by the harbor wall.

Seven towns each give an examination mark. Passing the Grand Audit in Aldermoot gives the eighth mark and restores Tallyport's council seat. The guild assigns ordinary work in all eight towns. For example, a wagoner finds a shipment that never got billed. Work on orders and invoices locates more cases and their largest account, and Petra can submit checked draft bills. These are optional local jobs. The Trials also lead through towns that hold pieces of Ilva's investigation. Her letter addresses anyone rescued as she was and gives them the towns table. Her journal and the people who remember her give that investigation a human history. The letter and first vault establish that the stranger can find more records in the lower vaults.

Ilva spent years looking for other rescues and reports of lights at sea. On her last journey she also found evidence that council officials had struck inconvenient records from the public archive. Clerks kept independent copies and a log before doing so. Ilva asked Ysolt for a pen that transfers a verified entry back into the archive, then restored three entries on her own in Aldermoot. Her own rescue was a private deletion she made long ago. It was still visible in the independent log. Recovering the log brings the stranger to Ilva, who explains what she did and why. Once the candidate has both the evidence and a place at the council, Petra can put the restored entries and removal log before the council. Maren wants a public account recorded in the minutes. The council agrees to open the archive for inspection. The public and private stories can reach their resolutions in either order.

The last Great Truth puts Ilva's lost row back into the results long enough to compare it with the lights. The player learns the date of their own light and can go down to Tallyport to decide whether to row out or stay. Ilva stayed after her own light came. She wants the player to be able to make that decision openly. The same people see the player off or welcome them back from the pier. Tallyport's representation and the public inquiry do not depend on the personal choice.

## Rules of the world

| Subject | Canon and limits |
|---|---|
| The Ledger | The Ledger connects written queries to skilled work in this world. The item, creature, and choice of ability still matter. A correct answer can improve the chance of a hit without producing a guaranteed hit. A player's Ledger also provides the screens for working with and organizing records. |
| Company books | An ordinary trading guild keeps accounts, deliveries, inventories, and bills. Staff in the towns teach with their working copies and use narrower lists for practical tasks. Some tradespeople also clerk for the guild, and some factors study with a Circle master. Dates such as June 30 in the company fixtures are the closing date on a copy. |
| Practice and writes | The Marigold `manifest` is a separate, three-row teaching copy with `name`, `quantity`, and `destination` columns. Reading and assembling a query prepares the player for six typed tasks. An explicit handoff introduces the full company tables after the short manifest. A safe ability test on a dummy precedes ordinary combat. A SQL exercise, ability, or report can use its own snapshot. Practice writes and reports leave the guild's real copy alone. Approved clerks can take a checked draft from the workshop to the original records as a story consequence. |
| Historical archive | The lost tables belong to the older public record. Vault scrolls add table copies to the player's Ledger. Custodians, locked rooms, and council decisions made the originals inaccessible. The surviving rows are a record of what was written down, with gaps and differing copies. They can show a name, a date, and an association. A query alone cannot prove an intention, a cause of death at sea, or what a witness could not see. |
| Erasure and repair | Hiding a row from a public book denies people access to that record. People and events still exist. The independent removal log preserves the original name or date and names the writer responsible. Ysolt's pen copies an entry back using verified surviving evidence. It has no force over the past or the sea. Ilva remains in the `people` table throughout. Only her rescue row in `strangers` is missing. |
| Circles and Trials | A Circle is a level of SQL taught by the local master. An Examiner is the regional trial authority. Varrick Stone and six other Examiners give seven marks, then the council itself conducts the Grand Audit and grants the eighth. The master teaches, the Examiner tests, and a dungeon keeper guards a lost register. These roles are distinct, including the Lich and Crystal Sentinel in Kell. |
| Other competitions | A dojo tournament is optional and separate from the Trials. Sorrel is on that circuit. The eight regional rivals seek their own recognition through the Trials or make money and contacts from duels. A city can be visited without any dialogue elsewhere having happened. |
| Calendar and choice | `FOUND_ON` is June 14, 2026. `RETURN_ON` is July 24, forty days after rescue. The first opening scenes take place before the Marigold docks. The light's date is a date in the story, and the game opens the scene once the evidence is ready. Play and saves have no calendar deadline for making the choice. The historical outcome of the selected ending and the ability to continue playing the saved world are separate. |

The guided first day keeps the saved guide step indices and records the lesson checkpoints separately. The opening award is 25 gold the first time a character completes the lesson. Reopening or replaying a finished lesson gives no second award.

## Timeline

| Date | Event and evidence |
|---|---|
| 1247–1866 | Seven confirmed historical rescues on the Brass Sea. Different vessels rescue the survivors and three vessels share the name Marigold. Each rescue joins to a strength-12 storm starting two days earlier and a later light forty days after the rescue. Captains and departure dates appear in the `crew` and `voyages` records. |
| 1622-05-01 | Council clerk Sabina Vale removes Jory Penn's entry. Penn's name and birth year are retained in the log and available through an independent copy. |
| 1702-09-12 | Councillor Jonas Rusk removes the Red Oar voyage entry for 1688-04-04. Another dispatch register still contains that departure. |
| 1911-02-02 | Council clerk Elsie Burr removes Sten Ash's entry. The log and another town's roll identify him. Ilva learns of the three removals by following conflicting copies. |
| 1986-03-02 | Wenna Tull captains the Marigold that brings Ilva in. Odalys teaches Ilva and Hesper sparrs with her at the Tallyport dojo. |
| 1986-04-10 to 1986-04-11 | Ilva removes her stranger record one day before the light she later reports. She had begun to trust people here and was frightened that admitting she could leave would invite someone else to make that decision. She sees the light and stays. |
| 1990 to 2012 | Pell ferries Ilva in 1990, Sela checks the ship lists with her in 1992, and Gus spars with her in 1993. Ness casts a first nib in 2002 and it cracks. Aurel points Ilva to independent clerks' records in 2012. These are optional memories. |
| Spring 2026 | Ilva leaves a letter in the harbor tower and deposits journal pages as she travels. Haverly counts the omissions, Four Fords identifies a missing voyage, and Ysolt tests a repair pen on an independently verified entry. The Lich helps Ilva find the removal log and Calla gives her the watch records. Ilva restores three public entries in the archive below Aldermoot. |
| 2026-05-31 | Corin dies during his expedition in the Bilge Vaults. The watch remembers his borrowed lamp and First Mate Vale remembers his chalk trail ending at the keeper's door. This is two weeks before the stranger's rescue. Corin had entered the vault to follow the ships list. |
| 2026-06-12 to 2026-06-17 | A strength-12 storm starts on June 12. A light is logged June 13. Tamsin and Captain Bram Tull's crew pull the stranger aboard on June 14. They land three days later. The `voyages` history lists a June 10 sailing to Tallyport. |
| 2026-07-24 | Forty days after rescue, Bram's watch record gives the light off Tallyport. This date is derived from `FOUND_ON`. The stranger can choose what to do after completing the evidence. |

## The principal cast

| Person | Wants, difficulty, and how they speak |
|---|---|
| Tamsin | Wants the stranger safe and able to look after themself when she goes back to duty. Lends a hand, pays attention, and will swear when she is scared. Her instinct is to fix the next immediate problem before either person has to explain why it matters. A companion at first, then someone the player can part from on purpose. |
| Odalys Brine | First Circle master and quartermaster. Afraid of seeing another pupil take the risks Corin did. She shows care by giving a person a useful job, asking them to show their work, and making a chair available. Training is more valuable to her than flattering talent. Seeing Tallyport's mark entered gives her room to respond with pride. |
| Ilva Thorne | Scholar, former sea stranger, Corin's teacher. She wants the council's victims to recover their public records and the next stranger to have a free choice about home. She can admit a breach in a record in writing years before admitting why she hid her own. At Aldermoot she tells the player why and separates the proof from her suspicions. |
| Corin Hale | The absent champion. Ilva taught him to check records and he went too far into a dangerous vault trying to bring back the ships table. Odalys remembers him bringing Ilva tea and leaving with homework. The people on watch remember his lamp and marks on the floor. His work provides a reason for the player to explore, with room to stop or go back. |
| Sorrel Ashby | Competitive duelist from Highwatch. She wants to know how good she is away from home. She gets defensive when she loses, then goes back to practise and learns to concede a good bout. She treats a rematch as an invitation to get to know someone. Beneath the confidence, a hometown bout matters to her more than she likes to say. |
| Bram and Wenna Tull | Two captains from the same family. Wenna rescued Ilva in 1986, Bram was the captain when Tamsin helped rescue the player. Bram keeps an honest watch record. Tamsin is the present-day contact and named rescuer the player gets to know. Bosun Marr and First Mate Vale remember the expedition. |
| Ysolt Karn | A capable smith who wants to understand the work her tools enable. She insists on seeing both copies before agreeing to make the repair pen. She is prepared to make a dangerous thing when the limits are clear, then doubts whether she asked enough. Her speech is specific about materials, evidence, and who holds responsibility. |
| Petra Voss | Public archivist. She wants to accept corrections she can defend and keep custody of the records clear. She leaves room for verified entries and worries about taking the originals outside the archive. When the evidence can survive public scrutiny, she brings it to the hearing herself. |
| Maren Holt | Highwatch master and guildmaster in the company records. She values a precise report because others act on it. Protecting the guild and the institution has made her cautious. When Ilva's evidence is present she wants public minutes so the council cannot quietly close the matter again. |
| Bryn Okoro | Eighth Circle master and company factor. Worries about executing a change nobody else can check. He demonstrates practice writes and hands clean drafts to authorized clerks, then asks candidates to take their completed work into the Audit. He treats properly proved evidence as part of that responsibility. |
| Council chair and clerks | A public body whose past officials could seal shelves and remove entries without challenge. Its first response is procedure: the required marks and the written record. Public restoration gives the living council a decision it can be held to. The known erasers are historical officeholders. The evidence does not name a current individual culprit. |

## Knowledge and payoffs

| Fact or promise | Who knows it and what unlocks a claim |
|---|---|
| Why Tamsin is helping, who Corin was | Tamsin names herself on the ship and explains Corin when the quay comes into view. Odalys, Wex, Varrick and the dungeon witnesses can supply their independent memories. A first meeting assumes only what that speaker observed or heard locally. |
| Why Ilva wrote to the player | Odalys has the letter. A journal page can be found before the letter and still reads as a dated entry. An optional witness can talk about Ilva without greeting the player as if they have already shown it. |
| Ordinary discrepancies and deliberate removals | A missing bill in the company records has ordinary consequences. The personal investigation has separate historical subjects. Ilva and her witnesses compare independent copies and find names and dates. The removal log identifies the authority behind each intervention. |
| Ilva hid her own rescue record | Ilva knows from the day she did it. Page six can introduce her admission. The eleventh Great Truth confirms the row and author from `people` and `erasures`. Her archive scene supplies the motive and her choice to stay. |
| Council accountability | Ilva restores the entries once she has a verifiable source. Get the independent record in the archive and complete the Audit to see it reach the current council. The opening of the archive and recording of the admission are the public consequence. Either order reaches the same consequence. |
| A route home | The letter establishes that Ilva found a lead, and the storms and lights supply a pattern. The final Truth requires the archive and the three relevant tables. It yields the same forty-day interval for Ilva and the player. The evidence predicts when the light appears. Going home and staying are personal outcomes. |

## Decision log

- 2026-11-22. Rescues and return lights have authored dates and traceable witnesses. The missing company invoices form a complete optional case. The council squarely faces the independent removal record once both the Audit and archive discovery are complete. The chronological distinction between Odalys, Ilva, and Corin, and the institutional distinction between examiner and keeper, carry through the revised dialogue.
