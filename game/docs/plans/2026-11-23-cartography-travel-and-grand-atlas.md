## Accepted design and implementation decisions

- Surface world and corner minimap begin black; walking reveals a permanent 14-tile radius per character. Surveyed purchased territory is muted; personally explored terrain has full colour. Landmarks require local discovery or a regional survey; secrets and dungeon interiors remain exploratory.
- Every town has a physical, walk-in cartographer with a recognizable compass sign, chart table, scroll racks/cabinet and regional decoration. Its regional wall chart can be consulted freely without granting ownership or permanently revealing distant terrain. The free chart supports a waypoint to any public regional destination.
- A regional map is a permanent per-character purchase. Prices start at 600 gold and rise by 300 per Circle (600–2700), a substantial repeatable-earnings goal. It reveals surveyed regional geography/public destinations and permits free repeat fast travel to public surface destinations only while physically in that region. Prior automatic visited-town travel is replaced. No fast travel during combat, lessons, dungeon expeditions or from map-shop previews. Ferries stay free and remain necessary between regions.
- One persistent waypoint guides the player along actual traversable ground, with sea crossings represented by a leg to the appropriate ferryman, then the next ground leg after landing. Show an unobtrusive edge arrow, destination/next-leg name and distance; clear at the target, permit replacement/cancellation, pause while in a separate-map interior/dungeon. Route computation is bounded/cached, never a world search every frame.
- Added by Casey during implementation: a small directional thumb joystick at the stage's bottom right owns touch movement. Inner displacement walks, outer displacement runs, a dead zone rests, and release/cancel/blur always stops movement. Empty-ground taps never move the character. Tapping a reachable object interacts; a distant target gives a move-closer hint without automatically walking. Keyboard arrows remain supported. Placement mode retains explicit plot selection on the canvas. Update relevant browser movement helpers to exercise real joystick input instead of removed tap-to-walk behavior.
- Further scope explicitly added by Casey: across ALL eight regions, buildings need distinct architectural layouts and purposes, regional service-specific interiors and the correct staff inside their premises. Healers belong in treatment buildings with beds/medical fittings. Walk-through doors visibly open/close with sound. Dojo floors/interiors use the same modular structural system. Reusable stairs connect usable upper rooms in public buildings, with safe entry/return and persistent indoor position. Add regional architectural variations rather than limiting improvements to Tallyport. Harbour basins use deep water; light-blue shallows follow natural shorelines, excluding quay and pier edges.
- Physical trail signs at road junctions, town approaches and ferry branches point along actual routes. Readable interaction panels name destinations and directions. Add capital district signs. Existing journal-page signposts retain their content/IDs.
- The Grand Atlas of Brasswake costs 30,000 gold, is sold only in Aldermoot after the purchasing character owns all eight regional maps, and becomes one shared household furnishing entitlement. Installing/moving it is a deliberate house action. Its substantial chart-table art must appear inside the selected house and be directly interactable. Global travel is available only while using the installed feature in that house; all eight regional maps are also required on the using character, avoiding a supporting-character bypass. No portable/global map-menu shortcut. House pads remain useful for returning home. Duplicate/stale clicks cannot charge twice.
- Existing characters retain towns, known landmark information, quests, items, homes and progress. Historical walked paths cannot be reconstructed; initialize local reveal near current/known town anchors and explicitly preserve recorded discoveries. Compact/versioned exploration data, all five slots, export/import and cloud replacement use the same normalization. Never confuse generation reachability (`m.seen`) with player exploration.
- Reuse current modular buildings and add a separate new art atlas for shop furnishings, compass signs, direction signs and the Grand Atlas. Preserve raw originals and generation settings; total image spending stays inside the existing $20 authorization (previous run estimated $1.218326). Use a small reviewed new sample first. No unrelated story/style changes.
